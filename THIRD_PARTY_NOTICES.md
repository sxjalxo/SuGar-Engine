> **One asset still needs its provenance pinned: `assets/models/Box.gltf`.** It is a copy
> of the Khronos glTF sample `Box`, which the Khronos sample repositories distribute under
> per-model terms (CC0 or CC-BY, depending on the model and the repository revision it came
> from). The exact revision it was taken from is not recorded, so this file states what is
> verifiable rather than asserting a license. Before a tagged release, either confirm and
> cite the upstream revision and its license, or replace it with a hand-written fixture like
> the other three — it is a cube, and the importer test does not need Khronos' copy.

## Reporting a problem with this file

If an entry here is wrong, incomplete, or attributes something to the wrong author, that
is a bug — please open an issue. If you are the author of a bundled component and want
the attribution changed, open an issue or email <sujalgarje@gmail.com> and it will be
corrected.
